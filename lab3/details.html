Comprehensive Bird Care Guide

Creating a safe environment for your feathered companion is key to a long and happy life together.

Popular Beginner Bird Species

Top Recommended Birds

  • Budgerigars (Budgies): Small, friendly, and easy for beginners to care for.
  • Cockatiels: Affectionate, crest-headed birds known for whistling tunes.
  • Lovebirds: Active and social parrots with vibrant colorful feathers.

Essential Housing Tips

  1. Select a cage large enough for full wing spread.
  2. Provide natural wood perches of varying diameters.
  3. Clean food and water dishes every single day.
